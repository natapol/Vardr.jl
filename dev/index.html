Vardr.jl

Vardr is a general-purpose framework for building digital twins on top of SciML.

A twin in Vardr has four parts:

  1. A TwinModel: any ODEProblem, for example one generated by ModelingToolkit, plus the solver used to advance it.
  2. An ObservationModel: what the sensors measure, y = h(u, p, t) + ε.
  3. A belief about the real system's augmented state z = [u; θ]: the model states u and any parameters θ estimated online. It is represented by an ensemble (EnsembleBelief) or weighted particles (ParticleBelief).
  4. An estimator that keeps the belief synchronized with data: EnKF, ParticleFilter, or NoAssimilation (open loop).

Where to start

  • New to Vardr? Follow the Tutorial: one twin, built step by step, in about 10 minutes.
  • Building your own twin? Follow the Step-by-step workflow, which goes from the question to deployment with a check at every step.
  • Working in pharma or bioprocessing? The Case study: a bioprocess twin applies the workflow to a fed-batch bioreactor and a risk-constrained feed decision.
  • Want to understand the methods? The Theory chapters derive what Vardr computes, with references: Digital twins and state-space models, Bayesian filtering and data assimilation, and Prediction, calibration and verification.
  • Using a twin for decisions: Forecasts and what-if scenarios.
  • Deploying: Running live and Calibration and persistence.
  • Something wrong: Troubleshooting.

Installation

Vardr is not yet registered. Install it from GitHub:

using Pkg
Pkg.add(url = "https://github.com/natapol/Vardr.jl")

You will usually also want Distributions (for priors), and ModelingToolkit if you write models symbolically.

Tips

  • Start Julia with threads (julia -t auto). Ensemble members are integrated in parallel.
  • Give estimated parameters a small process_noise, so their ensemble spread does not collapse and the twin can follow slow drift in the real system.
  • Particle filters need a prior that covers the truth and many more members than an EnKF.
  • Use an explicit RNG (rng = Xoshiro(seed)) for reproducible runs and exact snapshot/restore! round trips.
