Examples

The repository's examples/ directory contains complete, runnable twins. Each script simulates a "real system" with known ground truth, builds a twin from imperfect prior knowledge, and reports how well the twin recovers the truth. They are a good template for your own twin.

julia --project=examples -e 'using Pkg; Pkg.develop(path="."); Pkg.instantiate()'
julia --project=examples -t auto examples/thermal_room_twin.jl

Building your own twin: a recipe

A short version of the Step-by-step workflow:

  1. Model. Write f(u, p, t), or an MTK system, with named states and parameters. Simulate it and check that it behaves plausibly.
  2. Sensors. List what is measured, at what noise level. Mark gaps with NaN.
  3. Synthetic test. Choose "true" parameters that differ from your design values, simulate noisy data, and check that the twin recovers the truth. Compare it against NoAssimilation.
  4. Tune. Set the prior, process_noise, N and inflation until the errors are small and the uncertainty is honest (~95% of the truth inside ±2σ).
  5. Calibrate on historical data with calibrate!, if you have it.
  6. Replay history through run! with a TableSource, with sinks.
  7. Go live with a ChannelSource or your own source, a WallClock and a SnapshotSink.
  8. Use it. Add forecasts, alerts and what-if analyses for the decisions that matter.
